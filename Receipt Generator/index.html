Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about creating receipts with QuickReceipt.

Is this receipt generator free?

Yes. QuickReceipt is free to use, with no sign-up and no paid features.

Is my business or customer data uploaded?

No. Your details and draft are stored locally in this browser and are not sent to a server.

Can I print or save a receipt?

Yes. Print directly from your browser, or download a PDF or PNG copy.

How are tax and discounts calculated?

Discounts are taken off the subtotal before tax. Tax can be calculated as included in, or added to, item prices. Tips are not taxed.

Can I use these for official tax records?

Receipt and tax rules vary by location. Check your local requirements and keep the records required for your business.
