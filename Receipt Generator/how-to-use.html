Quick start guide

How to create a receipt

Build a polished receipt for a real sale in a few quick steps. Your preview updates as you enter details, and your draft stays in this browser.

1

Enter your business details

Add your business name and any address, contact details, tax ID, or logo you want to show on the receipt. Your details are saved in this browser for your next visit.

2

Set up the receipt

Check the receipt number and date, then optionally enter staff and customer details. The number is generated automatically and increments for new receipts.

3

Add the sale items

For each item, enter a description, quantity, and unit price. Use Add item for another line, Duplicate to copy a line, or the remove button to delete one. Line totals and subtotal update automatically.

4

Apply adjustments and payment

Choose a currency, and add any discount, tax, tip, or cash payment information. Turn on Prices include tax if your item prices already include tax. For card payments, you can enter the last four digits.

5

Review and style the preview

Choose Thermal, Classic, or Modern. Review the amounts, tax treatment, and payment details. Keep the SAMPLE watermark on while testing; turn it off for a receipt you are ready to issue.

6

Print, download, or copy

Choose Print receipt, Download PDF, Download PNG, or Copy plain text. For PDF and PNG downloads, your browser needs an internet connection to load the pinned export libraries.

A note about your records

Check your receipt before sharing it. Tax and receipt requirements depend on your location. QuickReceipt helps prepare a receipt but does not determine your tax obligations. Your receipt details stay in your browser; use Clear all data on the generator to remove saved business details and draft.
